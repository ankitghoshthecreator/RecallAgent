# NovaTech Project Registry

## Project: Atlas

Project ID: NT-P-201

Atlas is a machine learning platform developed by NovaTech.

The project focuses on automated model training, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring.

Atlas is maintained by the Research and Development department.

The technical leadership for Atlas is provided by Priya Mehta.

The primary engineering contributors include Rahul Sharma and Arjun Kapoor.

Atlas requires collaboration between machine learning engineering and software engineering teams.
