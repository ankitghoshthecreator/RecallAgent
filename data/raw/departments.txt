# NovaTech Department Directory

## Research and Development

Department ID: NT-D-01

The Research and Development department is responsible for developing new technologies, machine learning systems, experimental platforms, and engineering capabilities.

The department maintains the Atlas project.

Priya Mehta provides engineering leadership for teams working within the department.

The department includes engineers and researchers working on machine learning and software engineering initiatives.

## Finance

Department ID: NT-D-02

## Customer Operations

Department ID: NT-D-03

The Customer Operations department is responsible for customer support operations, service quality analysis, and operational analytics.

Meera Nair coordinates the Orion project.
