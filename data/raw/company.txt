# NovaTech Company Information

NovaTech is a technology company that develops software platforms and data-driven products.

The company has several functional departments including Research and Development, Finance, and Customer Operations.

Research and Development focuses on technology development and engineering projects.

Finance manages financial planning, forecasting, accounting, and business reporting.

Customer Operations manages customer support operations and service analytics.

NovaTech uses cross-functional project teams. Employees may collaborate with people from different departments depending on the requirements of a project.

The company encourages collaboration between engineering, financial, and operations teams when projects require multiple areas of expertise.
